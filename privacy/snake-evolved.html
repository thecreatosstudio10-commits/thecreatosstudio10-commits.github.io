Privacy Policy — Snake Evolved

Last updated: August 2026

Snake Evolved is developed by The Creatos Studio, Pune, India.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect any personal information from users. Snake Evolved is an offline game that does not require account creation or login.

Third Party Services

Snake Evolved uses the following third party services:

Google AdMob — Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device advertising ID and app interaction data for ad personalisation. For more information visit policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Billing — Used to process in-app purchases. We do not store any payment information. All transactions are handled by Google Play.

Unity Technologies — The game is built using Unity engine which may collect anonymous usage data. For more information visit unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

In-App Purchases

Snake Evolved offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase for ₹49. This is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not store credit card or payment information.

Advertising

Snake Evolved displays interstitial and rewarded advertisements served by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional and player initiated. You may purchase Remove Ads to disable interstitial ads.

Children

Snake Evolved is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention

We do not store user data on our servers. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
