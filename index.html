01 // Client work & portfolio

Selected productions

Delivered for clients in education, enterprise, retail and telecom. Client names withheld here and shared on request.

[ Open a row for build detail ]
01

Metaverse social platform

Multiplayer island world with avatar customisation, virtual land purchase and an in-world property economy.

Photon Fusion 2MultiplayerVR

Persistent multiplayer world built on Photon Fusion 2. Players spawn on a shared island and move between additively streamed environments — a café, a dressing room where avatars are customised, and a reception hall where land and property are traded.

The economy layer covers purchasable land parcels, houses, apartments and furnishing, settled in in-world credits and persisted across sessions. The most architecturally complex project in the portfolio: networked state, ownership and scene streaming all had to hold together at once.

02

5G VR showcase

Multiplayer 360° solar system built as brand advertising for a major Indian telecom operator.

Photon PUNMultiplayerVR

The viewer stands on a floating platform in space with the nine planets laid out radially around them in full 360°. Looking at a planet surfaces its name; selecting it travels the platform to a rendered surface — the barren terrain of Mars, the pyramids and aurora on Earth — with narrated description at each destination.

Networked on Photon PUN so several viewers could share the experience. Produced as campaign content for a 5G network launch.

03

Security operations in VR

An Elastic security platform rebuilt as a collaborative VR environment for a Dubai enterprise client.

UnityREST APIEnterprise XR

The client wanted their security operations centre to exist in VR so analysts could work inside it alongside colleagues. The full Elastic environment was integrated through its API and rendered in Unity — authentication, live error streams and threat monitoring all readable and actionable in headset.

Multi-user, so the room functioned as a shared workspace rather than a single-seat visualisation.

04

Retail kiosk platform

Interactive product kiosks for a national Ayurvedic retail brand, deployed across stores in India.

UnityBackend APIRetail

In-store kiosk software wired into the client's existing backend over API, with a three-tier permission model covering users, admins and super-admins.

Two voiced avatar presenters walk customers through the catalogue in their own voices, giving product descriptions and pricing, with purchase completed at the kiosk.

05

Science education simulations

3D physics and chemistry experiments for school students in grades 8 to 12.

Unity3DEducation

A library of interactive experiments replacing equipment students rarely get hands on. Animal and plant cells are explorable in 3D with every part labelled and described, and chemical reactions run as simulations students set up themselves.

06

Zoo AR companion

Proximity-triggered AR that loads the animal in front of you, with 3D model, audio and description.

ARBLE BeaconsAndroid

Bluetooth beacon proximity drives the whole experience: walk up to an enclosure and the phone loads that animal in AR, in 3D, with its name, description and calls. No scanning, no searching.

Built on indoor micro-location navigation intended to generalise to zoos, malls and other venues too small for GPS. Delivered as a working prototype ahead of the beacon hardware rollout.

07

Snake Evolved Own IP — live

2.5D isometric snake battle royale, live on Google Play in 176 countries.

Unity 6.3AndroidAdMob · IAP

Our own title, shipped end to end. Tick-based movement on an isometric grid with depth sorting, five distinct AI opponent strategies, five power-ups with an override system, and a shrinking-arena battle royale mode alongside classic play.

Full monetisation stack — AdMob rewarded and interstitial, in-app purchase, IL2CPP builds for ARM64 and ARMv7. View on Google Play
