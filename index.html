CONSULTING & SERVICES

Consulting first, build second.

Most engagements start as consulting — a technical opinion, an architecture review, a feasibility call before a budget gets committed. When it makes sense to build, we build. Five years of shipping real projects across VR, mobile, and desktop.

🧭
Technical Consulting

Architecture reviews, engine and platform decisions, scoping and estimation. An honest read on whether an approach will hold up before you commit a budget to it.

🥽
XR Strategy & Feasibility

Whether a VR or AR idea is buildable, on which headset, at what cost. Device selection, interaction design, and realistic scope for Meta Quest and PC VR.

📊
Performance & Code Audits

Profiling Unity projects that ship slow or crash on target hardware. Draw calls, memory, build size, frame pacing — with a prioritised fix list you can hand to your own team.

🚀
Release & Monetisation Setup

Play Store submission, keystore and build configuration, AdMob and IAP integration, policy compliance. The unglamorous work that stalls launches.

🎮
Unity Game Development

Full-cycle game development for Android, iOS, PC, and WebGL. 2D, 3D, and 2.5D isometric. Multiplayer via Photon Fusion and Unity Netcode.

🕶
VR / AR Development

Immersive experiences for Meta Quest, Oculus, and PC VR. Training simulations, educational content, and interactive product demos. XR Interaction Toolkit.

📱
Mobile Apps & Games

End-to-end Android and iOS delivery including Play Store submission, AdMob monetisation, and in-app purchase integration.

🖥
Desktop SaaS (Windows)

C# WPF applications with Firebase backends and Next.js dashboards. Kiosk-style software, enterprise tools, and subscription billing.

⚙️
Unity Tools & Editor Extensions

Custom Unity Editor tools, asset pipeline automation, and Asset Store packages. Built for studios that need to move faster.
